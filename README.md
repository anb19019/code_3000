## Personal Statement on Ethics

### 1. Why

I value continuous learning because technology is constantly evolving, and staying informed allows me to make better, more responsible decisions. I also strongly value communication, especially with younger generations of engineers. By breaking down complex ideas and making the field more approachable, I hope to inspire others and reduce barriers to entry. These values align with the broader purpose of engineering: to improve lives, solve problems, and serve society—not just a small, privileged group.

### 2. What

I try to keep improving my skills and understanding, not just for myself but so I can use them to help others. I make an effort to explain ideas in simpler ways when I can, and I want to support and encourage people who are newer to engineering. I also try to think critically about the impact of what I build, especially who it might help or unintentionally harm.

### 3. How

I plan to regularly reflect on my decisions and ask myself if I’m acting in line with what I believe. I will seek feedback from others, especially people with different perspectives, and be open to changing my approach if needed. I also want to stay involved.
